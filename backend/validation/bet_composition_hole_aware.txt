BET-RANGE COMPOSITION BY THE HOLE-AWARE LABELLER (P2.8 measurement, NOT adopted)
  command : scripts/measure_bet_ranges.py with bet_category on poker.math.classify
            (value = hole_tier >= PAIR, draw = flush draw / open-ender / gutshot of the hole)
  date    : 2026-10-21
  result  : the coach keeps classify_legacy for the villain model (villain_model.bet_category)
            and the P1.12 BET_COMPOSITION. Owner decision pending.

Why not adopted. The v1 bots choose bets with classify_legacy, so they bet board pairs as
pairs. The hole-aware labeller files those bets as air, and the air share is pooled over all
boards, so on an unpaired board the conditioned range then holds bluffs the bot does not
have (a nit's river air goes from 1.5% to 12.9%). With this table and labeller patched in,
these known-spot invariants fail (tests unchanged, as the plan requires):
  test_coach_spots::test_bluffcatch_flips_on_villain_bluff_rate   (nit: Call. instead of Fold.)
  test_coach_spots::test_bluffcatch_flips_on_price                (maniac pot bet: Call.)
  test_implied_spots::test_reverse_implied_folds_a_dominated_hand
The villain model describes the v1 bots, so it labels their bets with their own labeller;
the hero's hand read (coach, review) is hole-aware. Revisit with the v2 bots (phase 3), or
condition the composition on board pairing.
