  Findings for phase-4 calibration (reported, not tuned: no code bug found behind them):
  - WTSD is 40-52% for every type, 10-14 points over the §4 bands (known since P3.14).
  - TAG, LAG and Maniac are tighter than §4: lower VPIP, PFR and especially 3-bet (TAG 4.5,
    LAG 5.5, Maniac 6.7). The TAG never limps, so its VPIP-PFR gap (4.8) is all flats and
    BB calls.
  - Regs over-fold the river by about 20 points at every size. That follows from the D34
    river priors: the bettor's bluff share is 0.27 at 3/4 pot, 0.26 at pot and 0.34 at
    1.5x, each below a pure bluff-catcher's price (0.30, 0.33, 0.375), so the policy folds
    bluff-catchers. Under these priors the reg river-fold bands can't hold. That is a
    decision for the owner: raise the river bluff priors, or accept regs who over-fold the
    river.
  - The Station never folds to a BTN open from the BB, or to a 3-bet after opening: its
    call width (2.6×) is more than 100% of hands.
  - Win rates (Classic lineup, no rake) are not the §5 economy: TAG +22.4 bb/100 (band
    -2 to +10 after rake in the Realistic pool).
